## Slide 01 — 两个相邻闭环将客户洞察转化为可问责的行动组合——从证据到行动

```slide-meta
id: bilingual-customer-value-cycles
kind: content
section: operating-model
structure: explore-converge-cycles
explore_converge_cycles:
  cycles:
    - id: customer-needs
      label: 探索客户需求 / Explore customer needs
      emphasis: focal
      inputs:
        - 客户旅程、服务接触点与行为数据中的信号 / Signals from journeys, service contacts, and observed behaviour
      exploration_scope:
        - 跨客群与关键时刻验证优先机会假设，并识别决定价值的证据缺口 / Test priority opportunity hypotheses across segments and moments
      convergence_outputs:
        - 具有价值、可行性与证据基础的机会主题 / Evidence-backed opportunity themes with value and feasibility implications
    - id: priority-actions
      label: 收敛优先行动 / Converge priority actions
      emphasis: standard
      inputs:
        - 已验证的机会主题、交付约束与关键依赖关系 / Validated opportunity themes, delivery constraints, and dependencies
      exploration_scope:
        - 在投入、责任、节奏与执行风险之间解决取舍 / Resolve trade-offs across investment, ownership, sequencing, and execution risk
      convergence_outputs:
        - 具备负责人、决策节点与近期交付里程碑的行动组合 / Agreed action portfolio with owners, decisions, and milestones
  handoff:
    id: opportunity-to-action
    label: 将已验证机会转化为可问责行动 / Translate validated opportunities into accountable actions
    from_cycle_id: customer-needs
    to_cycle_id: priority-actions
```

**Key Message**

第一个闭环以客户证据确定真正重要的机会；相邻的第二个闭环将其转化为可执行、可问责的优先行动组合。
