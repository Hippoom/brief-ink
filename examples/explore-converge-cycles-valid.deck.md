## Slide 01 — Customer evidence becomes accountable action through two linked cycles

```slide-meta
id: customer-value-cycles
kind: content
section: operating-model
structure: explore-converge-cycles
explore_converge_cycles:
  cycles:
    - id: customer-needs
      label: Explore customer needs
      emphasis: focal
      inputs:
        - Customer signals from journeys, service contacts, and observed behaviour
      exploration_scope:
        - Test priority opportunity hypotheses across customer segments and moments
      convergence_outputs:
        - Evidence-backed opportunity themes with clear value and feasibility implications
    - id: priority-actions
      label: Converge priority actions
      emphasis: standard
      inputs:
        - Evidence-backed opportunity themes and delivery constraints
      exploration_scope:
        - Resolve trade-offs across investment, ownership, sequencing, and execution risk
      convergence_outputs:
        - Agreed action portfolio with accountable owners and decision milestones
  handoff:
    id: opportunity-to-action
    label: Translate validated opportunities into accountable actions
    from_cycle_id: customer-needs
    to_cycle_id: priority-actions
```

**Key Message**

The first cycle establishes which customer opportunities matter; the adjacent second cycle converts that evidence into a practical, owned action portfolio.
