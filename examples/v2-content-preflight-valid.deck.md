## Slide 02 — Fragmented identity prevents coordinated customer activation

```slide-meta
id: fragmented-identity
kind: content
section: strategic-imperative
structure: narrative
content_positioning:
  audience_question: Why can the current model not deliver coordinated activation?
  desired_outcome: Leadership recognizes fragmented identity as the immediate operating constraint.
  story_role: explain
  relationship_model: single-idea
  proof_requirement: source-cited
  detail_level: executive
```

**Key Message**

Business-unit customer records cannot reliably support cross-business targeting, measurement, or frontline recognition.

**Content**

- Customers can receive overlapping offers from different business units.
- Cross-business behavior is not consistently visible to frontline teams.
- Loyalty value is measured within individual businesses rather than across the relationship.

**Footnotes**

1. Current-state assessment interviews and customer-data review, September 2026.
